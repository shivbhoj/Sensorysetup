Sensory Setup is a focused, high-trust “hub” for overwhelmed adults with ADHD, then layer in affiliate offers and your own products.

Below is a lean but complete plan you can actually execute.

***

## 1. Core Positioning

Audience:
- Late-diagnosed or self-suspecting adults (20s–50s), especially professionals and solopreneurs.
- Overwhelmed, shame-loaded, stuck in executive dysfunction, looking for “what actually works.”

Promise:
- “Zero-fluff ADHD help: short, practical tools that work with your brain, not against it.”

Differentiators:
- Built by/with ADHD adults, not just clinicians.
- Curated, opinionated recommendations (not giant link dumps).
- Everything is **actionable in under 5 minutes** per resource.

***

## 2. Site Structure (MVP Sitemap)

Top-level pages/sections:

1. Home
   - Quick self-identification (“Does this sound like you?”) with 5–7 bullets of common pain points.
   - Jump links to: “I need help with…” (Focus, Time, Emotions, Work, Relationships).

2. Start Here (Newly Diagnosed / Suspect ADHD)
   - Short explainer on ADHD in adults and how it impacts work, relationships, and self-esteem.[1]
   - Links to top introductory resources (ADDitude, CHADD, How to ADHD, Understood).[2][3][4]

3. Toolkit by Problem (your main value engine)
   Each “problem page” is monetizable and highly shareable:
   - Focus & Distraction
   - Time Blindness & Planning
   - Organization & Clutter
   - Emotional Overwhelm / RSD
   - Work & Career
   - Relationships & Communication

   On each problem page:
   - 3–5 sentence description of the pain (in Reddit language).
   - 3 tiers of resources:
     - “Do in 5 minutes” (micro-strategy or template).
     - “Use this tool/app” (affiliate apps, planners, etc.).[5][6][7][8]
     - “Deep dive if you’ve got capacity” (books, podcasts, courses).[3][9][10][11]

4. Apps & Tools Directory (High-Intent Affiliate Page)
   - Categorized list:
     - Routine & focus apps (Hero Assistant, Forest, TickTick, Habitica, Inflow, Tiimo, etc.).[6][7][8]
     - Planners, timers, fidget tools, physical organizers.[11][2][3][5]
   - Short opinionated blurbs: who it’s for, what problem it really solves.

5. Content Hub (Articles / Guides)
   - “How to build an ADHD-friendly routine.”
   - “Time blindness: 7 ways to see time again.”
   - “ADHD-safe ways to use Notion/Todoist/Google Calendar without overwhelming yourself.”

6. Find Help (Ethical, non-monetized or minimally monetized)
   - Directories & organizations: CHADD, ADDA, local support groups, therapist directories.[4][12][13][2]
   - Clear disclaimers (not medical care).

***

## 3. Content Angles that Match Real Pain

Tie articles directly to lived frustrations we saw in communities:

- “ADHD Activity Paralysis: Why You Sit There ‘Doing Nothing’ for Hours & How to Unfreeze.”
- “RSD: When One Text Ruins Your Whole Day (And What to Do in 10 Minutes).”
- “ADHD & ‘Wasted Potential’: A Framework for Rebuilding Self-Trust.”
- “Burnout Every 2 Months? An ADHD-Safe Energy Budget.”
- “Decision Paralysis with ADHD: How to Make ‘Good Enough’ Decisions Faster.”

Each article:
- Opens with 2–3 “this is me” bullets.
- Offers 1–3 simple, step-by-step interventions.
- Recommends 1–3 tools or products that truly help (your affiliate spots).[7][5][6][11]

***

## 4. Monetization Plan

Monetization pillars:

1. Affiliate content
   - Apps (Inflow, Tiimo, Hero Assistant, Focusmate, Forest, etc.).[8][3][6][7]
   - Physical tools (timers, whiteboards, planners, fidgets, organizational products).[2][3][5][11]
   - Books & workbooks (organizing, routines, ADHD coaching-style guides).[9][3][4]

2. Your own lightweight products (phase 2)
   - Notion / Google Sheets templates for ADHD routines, task dashboards, weekly review.
   - Short “micro-courses” (email or video) like “7-Day Time Blindness Reset.”
   - A paid “implementation library” that bundles step-by-step flows for different problems.

3. Services / lead-gen (optional)
   - If you want, offer 1:1 paid implementation or async “systems setup” for ADHDers.

Affiliate strategy:
- Create “Best X for Y” posts (search-friendly and conversion-friendly):
  - “Best ADHD Apps for Routine & Focus in 2026.”[6][7][8]
  - “Best ADHD-Friendly Planners & Timers for People Who Hate Planning.”[3][5][11][2]
- Use honest pros/cons, include “Who should NOT use this” to build trust.

***

## 5. Execution Roadmap (90 Days)

Phase 1 – Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
- Decide brand name, tagline, and visual style (calm, non-clinical).
- Set up:
  - Domain + fast, simple site (Webflow/Framer/WordPress).
  - Essential pages: Home, Start Here, Toolkit (just 2–3 initial problem pages), Apps & Tools (v1), About.

Phase 2 – High-Intent Content (Weeks 3–6)
- Publish:
  - 3 “Best X tools” articles (routines, focus, planners), each mapped to specific affiliate programs.[5][7][8][11][6]
  - 3 pain-based guides tied to those categories (activity paralysis, time blindness, emotional overwhelm).
- Add email capture with a simple lead magnet (e.g., “One-Page ADHD Daily Flow” PDF).

Phase 3 – Audience & Feedback (Weeks 7–12)
- Share content in ADHD subreddits & FB groups in a non-spammy way (answer questions, then link your guide when relevant).
- Add a 1-question poll on key pages: “What are you struggling with most right now?”
- Use responses to decide next guides and products.
